How rating works

Placement rewards (rating / points): 1st 100 / 500, 2nd 60 / 250, 3rd and 4th 30 / 100, 5th to 8th 20 / 50, 9th to 16th 10 / 25.

  • Rating never goes down. It only grows, so playing is always worth it.
  • Upset bonus: win a match against a higher-rated player (rating at the start of the tournament) and earn extra rating: +5 for a 100+ gap, +10 for 200+, +15 for 300+. Max +30 per tournament.
  • Attendance bonus: +5 rating for your 3rd and your 5th tournament in a week (Mon to Sun).
  • Most Wanted: beat the bounty holder to claim the bounty.
  • Tiers: Rookie, Iron 60, Bronze 150, Silver 300, Gold 500, then Master (top 10%) and Champion (top 2%).
Updates automatically after every tournament.
